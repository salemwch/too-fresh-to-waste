Collecting a Surprise Bag takes less than two minutes. Here's exactly what
happens from the moment you open the app to the moment you walk away with your
bag — and your points.

## Step 1 — Browse Available Offers

![Browse offers near you](/images/buy-screen-onoarding/home-screen.png)

Open the app and you'll see all available Surprise Bags near you. Each card
shows the business name, the type of food, the price, and the pickup window.
Scroll through, filter by distance or category, and tap the one that catches
your eye.

**Tip:** Popular listings from bakeries sell out quickly — check the app in the
early afternoon to grab the best spots.

## Step 2 — View the Offer Details

![Offer details and Reserve button](/images/buy-screen-onoarding/offer-details.png)

Tapping a listing opens the full details screen. You'll see the description,
what's typically inside the bag, the exact pickup window, and the address. When
you're ready, tap the **Reserve** button.

## Step 3 — Choose Your Quantity

![Select quantity](/images/buy-screen-onoarding/reserve.png)

A reservation sheet slides up asking how many bags you want. Select your
quantity and confirm. Payment is handled securely in-app — no cash needed at the
counter.

## Step 4 — Confirm Your Order at Checkout

![Checkout — pickup or delivery](/images/buy-screen-onoarding/checkout.png)

At checkout you can select **Pickup** (always available) or delivery where
offered. Review your order summary and tap **Confirm Order** to lock in your
reservation.

## Step 5 — Your Order Summary

![Order summary](/images/buy-screen-onoarding/order-summary.png)

Your order is confirmed. The summary shows what you ordered, the business name,
and your exact pickup time slot. You'll also see a code field — this is where
you'll enter the code you receive at the business when you arrive.

## Step 6 — Enter the Pickup Code

![Pickup code confirmation](/images/buy-screen-onoarding/code-order-summary.png)

When you arrive at the business, the staff will give you a code. Enter it into
the code field in your order summary and tap **Confirm Pickup**. This verifies
the handover on both sides.

## Step 7 — Collect Your Points

![Your loyalty points](/images/buy-screen-onoarding/MY-Points.png)

Points land in your account automatically once pickup is confirmed. Every bag
you rescue earns you points on the leaderboard.

The **top 5** users win a smartphone. The **next 5** win a smartwatch. Everyone
else in the top rankings wins a discount voucher between **10–15%** — so every
bag you collect brings you closer to a prize.

---

That's the full loop. Browse → reserve → pay → pick up → earn. The whole process
from app open to walking out with your bag takes under five minutes, including
the pickup itself.

Download the app and find what's available near you today.
