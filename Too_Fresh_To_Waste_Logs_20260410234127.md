## II. The Problem & The Solution

### A. The Problem: Environment and economic Problem

The Business Loss: Hotels, Restaurants, bakeries, Grocery, cafes and
supermarkets discard massive quantities of perfectly edible, high-quality food
simply because it reaches the end of the business day unsold. This represents a
total loss of revenue and resources.

The Consumer Struggle: Simultaneously, students, low-income families, and
price-conscious citizens face rising food costs and affordability challenges.

The Environmental Gap: This waste contributes to unnecessary CO2 emissions and
landfill pressure, yet no efficient, automated bridge existed in Tunisia to
connect this surplus with those who need it until now.

Too Fresh To Waste was born from a direct observation of daily food waste in
Tunisia. Restaurants, bakeries, hotels, and supermarkets discard large
quantities of perfectly edible food every day simply because it reaches
end-of-day without being sold. Meanwhile, students and low-income families in
the same neighborhoods face real food affordability challenges.

After 8 months of intensive, full-time research and development, I has built a
complete, production-grade three-tier digital platform that connects these two
groups. Merchants list their unsold daily surplus as “Mystery Bags” or “Specific
Items” at discounted prices , and consumers purchase and collect them directly
from the merchant's location.

The platform is not just a transactional marketplace — it is a gamified social
movement. A unique "Drop" system rewards the most active users every 30,000 bags
sold and a Maximum of 4 drops per Year , creating powerful viral engagement
loops. A personal 5% donation commitment from The founding team's commitment to
donating 5% of their personal income contributes to helping people in need of
food , school programs, and hospitals , a founding value, not a marketing
tactic.

### B. The Solution: The "Too Fresh To Waste" Ecosystem

After 8 months of full-time engineering, i have developed a production-ready,
three-tier digital platform that solves this inefficiency:

For Merchants: A dedicated portal to list unsold daily surplus as "Mystery Bags"
or "specific items" at a minimum of 35% and a maximums of 90% discount or a free
surprise bag

For Consumers: A high-end mobile application to discover and a system points to
make the user attractive to the app

For the Community: A gamified "social movement" driven by a unique
"Drop-Giveaway" system that rewards sustainability and the most active users

### E. Innovation Contributions

#### 1. Gamified Sustainability — "The Drop" System

Every 30,000 bags sold triggers a national Drop event (maximum 4 per year). The
top 5 users on the leaderboard win prizes — 2 smartphones (350–400 DT each) and
3 smartwatches (~150 DT each). All other leaderboard participants receive
exclusive 10-15% merchant discounts. This creates a viral acquisition loop
driven entirely by user behavior.

#### 2. Multi-Dimensional Points Economy

Users earn loyalty points through: daily login streaks, purchase streaks, and
verified reviews. This drives retention and daily engagement without relying
solely on paid advertising.

#### 3. Social Impact Layer — 5% Donation Commitment

A personal founding team commitment — not drawn from investor capital — sends 5%
of founder earnings to food donations, clothes donation , school supplies, and
medical support for vulnerable Tunisian families.

#### 4. Merchant Growth Engine

Every merchant's participation becomes a free marketing channel. The analytics
dashboard, featured listings, and city-wide user base actively grow partner
revenue — making Too Fresh To Waste a business partner, not just a fee
collector.

#### 5. Household Module (Planned — Phase 5)

A future feature allowing private households to list near-expiry home food for
local community sharing, extending the platform from B2C into a true C2C
community food network I want this brand to be a social Impact

discounted quality food a high-value proposition.
